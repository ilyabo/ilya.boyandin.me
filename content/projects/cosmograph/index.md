Cosmograph is an online SaaS application for visualizing and analyzing large network graphs and machine learning embeddings based on [SQLRooms](/p/sqlrooms). It is developed by [Nikita Rokotyan](https://rokotyan.com/) and his team. I contributed to the SQLRooms integration and the web app development.
