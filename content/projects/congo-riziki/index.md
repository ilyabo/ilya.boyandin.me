Updated version of the [Congolese minerals data platform](/p/cobalt) made for [Resource Matters](https://www.resourcematters.org/). From mineral production and company payments to employment, environmental and social impacts, and the supply chains that connect Congolese resources to global markets, Congo Riziki brings together public data and records to support a better understanding and assessment of how the mining sector in the Democratic Republic of Congo operates, which companies are involved, and how their activities affect people and communities.
